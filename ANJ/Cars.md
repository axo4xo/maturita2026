### 4. The 4-Stroke Engine Process

You have this nailed down perfectly. Just say it exactly like this at the exam:

- **Stroke 1: Intake**
    
    - The piston moves down the cylinder.
        
    - It draws a mixture of fuel and air through the intake valve.
        
- **Stroke 2: Compression**
    
    - The piston moves upwards, compressing the mixture.
        
    - The valves are closed.
        
- **Stroke 3: Expansion**
    
    - The mixture is ignited by a spark plug.
        
    - The mixture explodes and pushes the piston downwards.
        
- **Stroke 4: Exhaust**
    
    - The exhaust valve opens.
        
    - The piston moving upwards forces exhaust gases out of the expansion chamber.
