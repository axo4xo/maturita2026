# My Specialisation
### 1. What I study

- I study at the **Secondary Industrial School in Liberec** (SPŠ Liberec, "Průmyslovka").
- My field is **Information Technology** (Informační technologie).
- The course is **4 years long** and ends with the **maturita** (state graduation exam).
- After graduation I receive a **vysvědčení o maturitní zkoušce** + can continue at university or start working in IT.

### 2. Why I chose this field

- I’m interested in **computers, software and technology** since childhood.
- IT is a fast-growing field with **lots of job opportunities** and decent salaries.
- It allows me to be **creative** (programming, design) and **logical** (problem solving) at the same time.
- I can work from almost anywhere – many IT jobs offer **remote / hybrid** work.

### 3. Main subjects

- **Programming** – algorithms, data structures, OOP; languages like Python, Java, C#, JavaScript.
- **Web development** – HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Node.js, databases.
- **Computer networks** – TCP/IP, routing, switching, Wi-Fi, security, Cisco basics.
- **Databases** – SQL, design, normalisation.
- **Electrical circuits and electronics** – Ohm’s law, components, circuits, soldering.
- **Operating systems and hardware** – Windows, Linux, troubleshooting, assembly of PCs.
- **Automation and robotics** – PLC, microcontrollers (Arduino, Raspberry Pi), sensors and actuators.
- **Computer graphics** – raster vs. vector, Photoshop, Illustrator, video editing.
- **Measurement and metrology** – measuring instruments, errors, sensors.
- **Safety and reliability** – data security, backups, error handling.

### 4. Skills I have learned

- **Programming** in several languages.
