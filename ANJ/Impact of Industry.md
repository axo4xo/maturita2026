# Impact of Industry
### 1. What "industry" means

- **Industry** = the production of goods, raw materials and services on a large scale.
- Includes **manufacturing, mining, energy, construction, agriculture (industrial), tech**.
- Industry shapes the **economy, jobs, environment and society**.

### 2. Sectors of industry

- **Primary** – raw materials: agriculture, mining, forestry, fishing.
- **Secondary** – manufacturing, construction, energy production.
- **Tertiary** – services (transport, retail, healthcare, IT).
- **Quaternary** – research, development, knowledge-based (R&D, IT, biotech).

### 3. Major Czech industries

- **Automotive** – Škoda Auto (Mladá Boleslav), Hyundai (Nošovice), TPCA / Stellantis (Kolín). The largest industry in CZ.
- **Engineering / machinery** – ČKD, Doosan Škoda Power.
- **Glass and crystal** – Preciosa (Liberec), Moser.
- **Brewing** – Pilsner Urquell, Budvar, Staropramen.
- **Electronics** – Foxconn (Pardubice), Onsemi.
- **IT and software** – Avast, Kiwi.com, JetBrains (Czech roots).
- **Energy** – ČEZ (Temelín, Dukovany nuclear plants).

### 4. Positive impacts of industry

- **Jobs** – creates employment for millions.
- **Economic growth & exports** – cars, machinery, electronics.
- **Cheaper goods** thanks to mass production.
- **Technological progress** – innovation, research, automation.
- **Infrastructure** – roads, railways, energy grids.
- **Higher living standards.**

### 5. Negative impacts on the environment

- **Air pollution** – smog, particulate matter (PM 2.5, PM 10), nitrogen oxides, sulfur dioxide.
- **Water pollution** – chemicals, heavy metals, oil spills, plastic.
- **Soil pollution** – mining, fertilizers, pesticides, industrial waste.
- **Greenhouse gases (CO₂, methane)** → climate change, global warming, melting ice caps, extreme weather.
- **Ozone layer damage** (CFCs, mostly banned by the Montreal Protocol).
- **Deforestation** – land cleared for agriculture, mining, paper production.
- **Loss of biodiversity** – animals lose habitat, plants and species become extinct.
- **Noise and light pollution.**

### 6. Climate change

- Caused mainly by **burning fossil fuels** (coal, oil, gas).
- Results: rising temperatures, sea-level rise, floods, droughts, wildfires, hurricanes.
- **Paris Agreement (2015)** – global goal to limit warming to **+1.5 °C** above pre-industrial levels.
- EU goal: **climate neutrality by 2050**.

### 7. Resource depletion

- **Fossil fuels** – limited supply, will eventually run out.
- **Rare-earth metals** – essential for electronics and EV batteries, often concentrated in few countries.
- **Fresh water** – becoming scarce in many regions.
- **Forests, fish stocks, fertile soil** – over-exploited.

### 8. Industry & society

- **Job loss** to **automation and AI**, especially routine jobs.
- **Globalisation** moves production to cheaper countries → social impact in old industrial regions.
- **Working conditions** – fair wages, safety, child labour in some countries.
- **Health effects** – respiratory diseases in industrial regions (e.g. Ostrava in CZ).

### 9. Solutions / sustainable industry

- **Renewable energy** – solar, wind, hydro, geothermal.
- **Energy efficiency** – better insulation, LED lighting, smart grids.
- **Circular economy** – reuse, repair, recycle, design for longevity.
- **Electric vehicles** instead of combustion engines.
- **Carbon capture and storage** (CCS).
- **Stricter environmental regulations** (emissions limits, carbon tax, EU ETS).
- **Green / sustainable buildings.**
- **Sustainable agriculture** – less pesticide use, bio products.

### 10. Industry 4.0 / Industry 5.0

- **Industry 4.0** – automation, IoT, robotics, AI, big data, smart factories.
- **Industry 5.0** – focus on cooperation between humans and intelligent machines, plus sustainability and resilience.
- CZ is heavily involved in Industry 4.0 – many universities and companies research it.

### 11. What individuals can do

- **Reduce, reuse, recycle.**
- **Save energy** at home (LEDs, insulation, smart heating).
- **Use public transport or bikes** instead of cars.
- **Buy local and seasonal** food.
- **Eat less meat** (especially beef).
- **Support sustainable brands and second-hand shopping.**
- **Avoid single-use plastics.**

### 12. Useful vocabulary

- Manufacturing, factory, plant, supply chain, mass production.
- Emissions, pollution, smog, greenhouse gases, carbon footprint.
- Renewable / non-renewable energy, fossil fuels, sustainability.
- Automation, robotization, AI, smart factory.

### 13. Speaking strategy

Start with what industry is and the four sectors. Mention 2–3 key Czech industries. List positive impacts (jobs, growth) then negative ones (pollution, climate change, resource depletion). Talk about Industry 4.0 / 5.0 and renewable energy as solutions. Finish with what an individual can do.
